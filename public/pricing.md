# Source.io Pricing & Plans

> Simple pricing for students, researchers and lifelong learners.
> A real free tier. No credit card required to start.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free ($0) | Scholar Pass ($9/mo) | Your own API key ($0) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Monthly price** | **$0** | **$9** | **$0** (you pay your AI provider, often free) |
| **Payment required** | None | Card, cancel anytime | None |
| **Daily AI actions** | 25 AI actions per day (resets 00:00 UTC) | 200 AI actions per day | No daily cap on generations that run on your key |
| **Documents kept** | 3 documents | 100 documents | 3 documents |
| **Pages per document** | Up to 25 pages, every page used | Up to 50 pages, every page used | Up to 300 pages |
| **Upload size** | 50 MB | 100 MB | 50 MB |
| **Audio recaps per day** | 2 | 50 | 2 |
| **Study modes** | All 5 (Notes, Flashcards, Quiz, Audio, Chat) | All 5 | All 5 |
| **Source citations in chat** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Spaced repetition** | Leitner 5-box review schedule | Leitner 5-box review schedule | Leitner 5-box review schedule |
| **Markdown export** | Yes | Yes | Yes |

One AI action = one generation (a set of notes, a flashcard + quiz set, an audio script, or one chat answer).
A "page" is measured as about 3,000 characters of extracted text, so pasted text and transcripts follow the same rule as PDFs.

## Plan Details

### 1. Free ($0)
- **For**: anyone with a document to learn.
- **Allowance**: 25 AI actions per day, replenished at 00:00 UTC.
- **Storage**: 3 documents, up to 25 pages each.
- **Includes**: all 5 study modes.

### 2. Scholar Pass ($9 / month)
- **For**: heavy users — full-time students, researchers, exam candidates.
- **Allowance**: 200 AI actions per day and 50 audio recaps per day.
- **Storage**: 100 documents, up to 50 pages each, 100 MB uploads.
- **Terms**: $9 per month. Cancel anytime from the billing portal; access continues until the end of the paid period.

### 3. Your own API key ($0)
- **For**: people who already have, or are happy to create, an AI provider key. A free Google Gemini key from Google AI Studio takes about two minutes.
- **Supported providers**: Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI.
- **How it works**: add the key under Settings. Generations that run on your key do not count against the daily limit, and documents can be up to 300 pages. Your provider's own terms and rate limits apply.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

### Can I try Source.io without entering payment details?
Yes. Create an account and start uploading immediately. No card needed.

### What happens when I use all 25 daily actions on the Free plan?
They reset at 00:00 UTC. You can also add your own API key in Settings, or upgrade to Scholar Pass.

### Are my documents used to train AI models?
Source.io does not sell your documents or train its own models on them. To generate study material, document text is sent to the AI providers listed in the Privacy Policy; long documents may be processed by Google Gemini, whose free tier may use inputs to improve Google's products. If you add your own API key, your provider's terms apply instead.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel any time from the billing portal linked in Settings → Plan & Billing. You keep Scholar access until the end of the current billing period, then return to the Free plan.
