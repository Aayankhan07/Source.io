## Detailed Plan Descriptions

### 1. Free Forever Plan ($0)
- **Who it is for**: Students, independent readers, and lifelong learners studying course material, research preprints, and exam notes.
- **Allowance**: 25 AI study generations every 24 hours (automatically replenished nightly at 00:00 UTC).
- **Storage**: 3 concurrent document slots.
- **Includes**: Full access to all 5 study modes (Structured Notes, 3D Leitner Flashcards, Practice Quizzes with explanations, Audio Recaps, and Grounded Source Chat).
- **Cost**: Completely free forever. No credit card, trial expiration, or surprise charges.

### 2. Scholar Pass ($9 / month)
- **Who it is for**: Full-time university students, graduate researchers writing dissertations or comprehensive literature reviews, and medical/legal candidates preparing for board exams.
- **Allowance**: Unlimited AI generations with priority queue dispatch.
- **Storage**: Unlimited active document slots and full-book ingestion (up to 300 pages per file).
- **Includes**: High-bitrate dual-voice studio audio podcasts, custom Leitner review curves, and full passage coordinate inspection.
- **Terms**: $9/month billed monthly. Cancel anytime with 1-click in account settings.

### 3. Developer / BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Mode ($0)
- **Who it is for**: Developers, AI enthusiasts, and technical researchers with existing API accounts.
- **Supported Providers**: Google Gemini API, Groq (Llama 3.3 70B), and OpenAI.
- **How it works**: Enter your API key securely under `/app/settings`. All requests execute directly against your own API quota with zero Source.io daily action limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

### Can I try Source.io without entering payment details?
Yes. You can create an account and immediately start uploading documents and generating notes without entering any credit card or billing information.

### What happens when I hit the 25 daily actions on the Free plan?
Your quota resets automatically every day at 00:00 UTC. If you need immediate additional generations, you can enter your own free Gemini or Groq API key in Settings, or upgrade to Scholar Pass.

### Are my uploaded documents or notes used to train commercial AI models?
No. Documents and audio uploads are processed in isolated sandboxed sessions with strict zero-retention policies. Your private intellectual work and course materials are never used for AI model training.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your Scholar Pass at any time from your account settings. You will retain unlimited access until the end of your current billing period, after which your account reverts to the Free Forever tier.
